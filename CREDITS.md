# Credits

Other mods this mod works with. Their files are not changed and not included here: our code reads their data or wraps their functions at runtime, and does nothing when the mod is not installed.

| Mod | Author | What our code does | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Passive Skill Trees (Rep+)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3305869734) | Ab Aeterno | trees are added through its API (AddCharacterTree, getTreeSnapshotMod, getLocalized) | `pst_talents.lua, pst/*.lua` |
| [Fiend Folio: Reloaded](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3778123093) | Erfly | trees for its characters; mechanics read its player data | `pst/ff_talents2.lua` |
| [Furtherance](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3513706025) | Benny | trees for its characters | `pst/fu_talents.lua` |
| [Lost and Forgotten](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3440586497) | ENFOR | trees for its characters | `pst/lnf_talents.lua` |
| [IPECAC Community Mod](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1925867065) | pedroff_1 | trees for its characters | `pst/ipecac_talents.lua` |
| [The Binding of Isaac: Epiphany](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3012430463) | The Epiphany Team | trees for its tarnished characters | `pst/ep_talents.lua` |

Library: [REPENTOGON](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3127536138) by jerb and the REPENTOGON team — required.

Thanks to the authors of these mods and to the whole Isaac modding community.
